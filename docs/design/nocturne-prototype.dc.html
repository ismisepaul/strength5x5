1Strength 5×5 — design review & Nocturne redesign
reviewWhat I'd change, and why

The current app is functionally excellent — smart deload logic, wall-clock timers, recovery flows. The UI works against it. Six findings, each applied in 1a:

1

Everything shouts. Nearly all text is 10px black-weight uppercase, so nothing has priority. Fix: hierarchy from size and space — one calm 500 weight, kickers only for section labels.

2

Five hues, unstable meanings. Indigo, emerald, rose, amber and blue share screens; rose means both "failed set" and "discard". Fix: one accent. A missed set is a number, not an alarm.

3

Radius roulette. 2.5rem cards, 2rem panels, 1.5rem buttons, xl chips, full circles — all at once. Fix: one 8–10px radius everywhere; set targets stay circular because they're a distinct control.

4

Nav hides mid-workout. The tab bar collapses to a hamburger during a session — the one time you may want the timer settings or program. Fix: nav stays; the rest timer docks above it, and a slim "live" bar follows you to other tabs.

5

Dead-slot noise. Unused set slots render as dashed ✕ boxes; adherence stats live only in the Log. Fix: render only real sets; surface streak + this-week right on the start screen where the decision happens.

6

Invisible gestures. Long-press-for-reps is undiscoverable and the tap-to-decrement cycle is unexplained. Fix: a one-line caption under the first exercise teaches both; the caption disappears after the first logged set.

Also worth doing (not shown): fold Program into Options to get a 4-tab bar; replace 8 full-screen modals with bottom sheets; keep FR/EN and Drive sync exactly as they are — they're good.
